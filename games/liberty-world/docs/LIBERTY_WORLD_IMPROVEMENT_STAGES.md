## Locked baseline

Cozy cartoon 3D is mandatory. Keep the original PCOCK, Liberty and Pulse Guy assets, blue/blue-purple sky with readable clouds, consistent green terrain family and separate warm paths. Reuse the current world, renderer, movement and minigame engines. Preserve story IDs, saved progress and rewards. PCOCK Runner, Ghost Route and Liberty Crossing remain included and optional. Most Wanted is outside this task.
