## The promise
A small peacock who has forgotten how to open his tail crosses Liberty Valley with a loyal friend. By helping its people choose their own paths, he restores seven colors, rebuilds a community and learns that freedom means making room for others.

## Liberty Valley
A walkable world of roughly 500 × 500 units surrounds Liberty Plaza. Villages and farms meet forests, ruins, a windmill, a lake with boats, rivers with repairable bridges, a coastal harbor and lighthouse, and mountain switchbacks, caves and Liberty's Summit. Product landmarks rise above their natural surroundings; roads, pulse lines and Feather Shrines connect them. Two optional Game Gates lead to Gasless Run and Ghost Route. Peg Keeper and mini-game expansion are on hold. Twenty-four residents follow a ten-minute day/night cycle; twelve side quests develop the named residents. Birds, fish, butterflies, weather and cosmetic reactions bring life between quests.

## Seven colors, one friendship
Hope → Safety → Growth → Stability → Courage → Independence → Anticipation. Each knowledge feather opens another part of PCOCK's tail and restores local color, then strengthens Liberty's torch and the pulse heartbeat. Story feathers remain distinct from spendable Feathers. Freedom grows through actions, rather than speeches.

## Voice and motifs
PCOCK communicates through chirps, head tilts, shivers and tail acting. Liberty is warm, dignified and quietly funny. Pulse Guy uses sparse slang; humor comes first, sincerity follows. Dialogue boxes contain at most two short sentences and three short lines. Folded/open tail, dim/bright torch, broken/rebuilt bridges and the pulse heartbeat recur. Nature keeps its own palette; Liberty structures and interfaces keep the approved brand.

## Emotional peaks and finale
After Growth, the Fee Storm overruns the plaza and Liberty's torch gutters. Pulse Guy stays beside PCOCK in a quiet friendship scene; staying is the victory. At the summit, the fictional Peg Engine switches on, the torch relights and the full tail opens. Color floods the Valley; a silent community flock gathers. Hold on the friends, then credits.
