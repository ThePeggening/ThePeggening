PCOCK: FEATHERS OF LIBERTY — LIBERTY VALLEY
Build: PCOCK-VALLEY-BETA-20261001-r9

PLAY LOCALLY
Extract this entire folder. With Node.js installed, open PLAY.cmd or run:
node tools/serve.mjs
Keep that window open and visit http://127.0.0.1:8080/pcock/ in your browser.
If port 8080 already hosts your game, use that address. To use another port,
set $env:PORT = '8081' in PowerShell before running the server.
Progress belongs to the browser and address; changing ports uses another save.
Opening index.html directly does not support module/model loading.

CONTROLS
WASD/arrows: move. Drag: look. Space: jump; hold while falling to glide.
Shift: hold to sprint. E: interact. J: journal. Escape: pause or hold to skip a scene.
Touch: movement stick, camera drag, Jump, Sprint and the interaction bubble.
During ring flights: jump once, then hold W or the forward stick. Wings catch
the updraft automatically. Return to launch perch is available after any miss.
H or Guide opens every activity. Follow the gold walking trail and destination ring.
Yellow rings show the correct answers and next action. Your guide saves its place.

GUIDED CINEMATIC ADVENTURE
19 main scenes with 62 shots, 12 neighbor epilogues, 6 discovery and 4 hunt scenes.
Next moment advances a scene; hold Escape or Hold to skip to skip it.
Detailed trees, house joinery, grass, stone, roofs, shores and landmark fittings.
All Academy lessons, plumes, shrines, sprint, signals and photos have guide entries.

SKILLS & SUPPLY QUESTS
Journal -> Skills & equipment shows Woodcutting/Fishing XP, levels and inventory.
Guide -> Skills & supply quests marks the grove, lake boardwalk and three
neighbor deliveries. E or tap starts three axe swings or a timed cast; move
away to cancel. Grove trees fall, leave stumps and regrow after 45 seconds.
Bronze/Iron/Steel axes and Reed/River/Master rods unlock at levels 1/2/4;

NPC prompts are positioned above faces. This build is the beta candidate.

START OVER
Title screen or Pause -> Start Over -> Start Over Again begins a fresh game.
R7 rebuilds the scene inside this page and opens PLAY at the beginning, even
when the address contains return=runner. Restore also works without navigation.
It resets story, wallet, collectibles, Feathers, XP and both mini-game records;
graphics, sound and controls remain. A backup is written before any reset.
Start Over -> Restore Previous Game restores the last backed-up game.
Keep playing cancels without changing progress. Starting over again replaces
the previous backup. Saves and this backup belong to this browser and address.
